# Sportsbook — Vertical Playbook

How to deploy Playbook across a sportsbook: the retail book, the app, the bet slip, and the game-day moments in between.

> **Operator note**: Sports bettors are the segment most confident in their own edge. They know the sport; the gap is the math of the market. The play here is respect first: never talk down to a fan, and never dismiss what they know. Add the piece they're missing (vig, line movement, why parlays pay the book) and they respect you back. See the [sports bettors segment](../messaging/player-segments/sports-bettors.md) before writing a word.

## The 30-second version

A sportsbook deployment lives inside the betting flow, not beside it. The bet slip, the odds board, and the in-play screen are the touchpoints that matter; a literacy line there beats a banner anywhere else. Content leads with the market math (vig, implied probability, parlay pricing) because that is the part of the game a sports fan hasn't already mastered. Time everything to the calendar: the big game, the season opener, and the tournament bracket are when attention peaks.

## The player journey

| Moment | Where | What runs there |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| Line shopping | Odds board, app home | Odds-format literacy: what -110 means and what the book keeps ([sports betting guide](../how-to-play/sports-betting.md)) |
| Building the slip | Bet slip | The RG line on the slip itself ([render: betslip](../collateral/render/)); parlay pricing shown plain |
| Pre-game | Push, social, lounge screens | Myth content timed to the matchup ([Myths 11 to 14](../messaging/myth-busting.md#myth-11-knowing-the-sport-means-youll-win)) |
| In-play | Live overlay | Pace-aware literacy lines in the in-play view ([mobile app touchpoints](../collateral/digital/mobile-app-touchpoints.md)) |
| Settlement | Result screen, email | Outcome framing that keeps wins and losses in entertainment terms ([email templates](../collateral/digital/email-templates.md)) |
| Between games | Social feeds | [Social media toolkit](../collateral/digital/social-media-toolkit.md) game-day content; quiz content for group chats |

The placement rule: meet the bettor inside the flow with one short line, and link out for depth. Nobody reads a brochure between the third and fourth quarter.

## Jurisdiction overlay

Sports advertising rules move fast and differ hard by market: bonus wording, odds display, in-stadium rules, and messaging windows around live sport. Pair this playbook with your [jurisdiction module](../jurisdictions/) and let mandated wording win every conflict. Modules tagged `sports` carry the betting-specific detail.

## Measure it

Score deployments with the [content scorecard](../docs/content-scorecard.md). The sportsbook tell is parlay literacy: when quiz answers on parlay pricing improve, the flagship campaign is working. Settlement-screen click-through to the guide is the clearest in-product signal.

## Start this week

1. Configure [_brand.yml](../_brand.yml) with your book's name and helpline.
2. Put the RG line on the bet slip from the [render set](../collateral/render/).
3. Publish the [sports betting guide](../how-to-play/sports-betting.md) in your app or content hub.
4. Time [Sports Betting Reality Check](../messaging/campaigns.md#campaign-3-sports-betting-reality-check) to the next big sports moment.
5. Brief the counter and support teams with the quick ref and scripts.

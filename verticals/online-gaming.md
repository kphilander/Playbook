# Online Gaming — Vertical Playbook

How to deploy Playbook in an iGaming casino: registration, deposit, lobby, in-session, and the email lifecycle around them.

> **Operator note**: Online is the vertical where you control every surface, so the bar is higher: no excuse for a literacy program that lives on one buried page. It is also the vertical where the two brand pillars pay off in product terms. Open means the RTP, the odds, and the bonus terms read plain. Social means the quizzes and myth-busters are built to share. Both are retention features, not compliance features.

## The 30-second version

Online deployment is lifecycle deployment. Every player passes the same gates: registration, first deposit, lobby, session, and the emails in between. Put one right-sized literacy moment at each gate and the program covers the whole base without a single interruption a player would call spam. The content hub does the depth; the product surfaces do the timing; the [embeddable widgets](../widgets/) do the work of keeping live odds and helpline blocks current everywhere at once.

## The player journey

| Moment | Where | What runs there |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| Registration | Sign-up flow | Tool-forward welcome: limits framed as a feature of a good product ([mobile app touchpoints](../collateral/digital/mobile-app-touchpoints.md)) |
| First deposit | Deposit flow | The deposit interstitial: plain bonus terms before the claim, not after ([Myth 20](../messaging/myth-busting.md#myth-20-a-bonus-is-free-money)) |
| Lobby | Game tiles, banners | App banners rotating game literacy; RTP where a player can find it ([render set](../collateral/render/)) |
| In session | Game frame | Session clock and reality-check moments written in Tier 1 voice, not alarm voice |
| Session end | Cool-down screen | The cool-down template: a full stop with warmth ([render set](../collateral/render/)) |
| Inbox | Email lifecycle | [Welcome, deposit, monthly, and reactivation emails](../collateral/digital/email-templates.md) each carrying one literacy beat |
| Content hub | Blog / help center | The [website content hub](../collateral/digital/website-content-hub.md) holding the full guide library |

The placement rule: one message per gate. A flow with three stacked disclosures reads as legal cover; one clear line at the right moment reads as the product talking.

### Myths that matter most here

The online pair from [myth-busting](../messaging/myth-busting.md): online slots are rigged (19) and a bonus is free money (20). The first is the trust myth: answer it with published RTP and regulator framing. The second is the fine-print myth: answer it with wagering terms in plain sight. Behind them, the slots set (Myths 1 to 6) transfers to online intact.

## Staff readiness

1. Support and chat agents work the [conversation scripts](../collateral/customer-service/conversation-scripts.md) and [staff FAQ](../collateral/customer-service/staff-faq.md); in chat, the script is the interface.
2. CRM owners check every lifecycle email against the [stigma-free language](../messaging/stigma-free-language.md) list.
3. Product owners read the [voice and tone chapter](../brand-book/04-voice-and-tone.md) before writing interstitial or reality-check copy; the difference between Tier 1 and Tier 2 voice is the whole game there.

## Jurisdiction overlay

Online rules are the most detailed of any vertical: mandatory reality checks, deposit-limit prompts, bonus wording, self-exclusion registry integration, and ad windows. Pair this playbook with your [jurisdiction module](../jurisdictions/); modules tagged `interactive` carry the product-level mandates. Where the law fixes exact wording, the template adapts around it.

## Measure it

Online gives you the cleanest measurement of any vertical: every placement has an impression count and a click-through. Score against the [content scorecard](../docs/content-scorecard.md), and watch two product signals: guide reads per active player, and voluntary limit adoption after tool-forward messaging. Rising literacy with steady retention is the whole thesis proved.

4. Run [Slots Myths Week](../messaging/campaigns.md#campaign-1-slots-myths-week) across app banners and email.
5. Put the [Game IQ quiz](../collateral/interactive/quiz-framework.md) in the lobby and measure completion.

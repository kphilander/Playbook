# Lottery — Vertical Playbook

How to deploy Playbook across a lottery: draw games, instant tickets, the retail counter, and the app.

> **Operator note**: Lottery is the widest-reach vertical in gambling and the lightest-touch: millions of casual players, a few seconds of attention at a counter, and a retail workforce you train at arm's length. The deployment that works is short, warm, and everywhere: odds in plain numbers, luck myths handled with a smile, and materials a busy clerk never has to explain. Playbook's voice fits lottery because lottery is already the entertainment end of the market; the job is keeping the math as visible as the jackpot.

## The 30-second version

Lottery deployment is reach deployment. The counter gets seconds, so counter materials carry one idea each: the odds, the helpline, or one myth. The app and email carry the depth. Jackpot surges are the calendar: when the top prize rolls and the queue doubles, the odds content should be at its loudest, because that is when the most people are doing the math wrong. Retailers are partners, not staff, so everything they touch has to work with zero training.

## The player journey

| Moment | Where | What runs there |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| The queue | Counter mat, screen loop | One-idea pieces: draw odds in plain numbers ([digital display specs](../collateral/environmental/digital-display-specs.md)) |
| Buying | Counter | [Rack cards](../collateral/print/rack-card.md) within reach; clerk materials that need no script |
| The ticket | Ticket-adjacent print | Helpline and odds statements per jurisdiction mandate ([helpline card](../collateral/print/helpline-card.md)) |
| Scratch play | Play stations | Instant-game myths: near misses and lucky stores ([myth-busting](../messaging/myth-busting.md)) |
| Draw night | App, social, email | Results framing plus one literacy beat ([social media toolkit](../collateral/digital/social-media-toolkit.md)) |
| Jackpot surge | Everywhere at once | The odds piece, loudest when the prize is biggest ([Myth 16](../messaging/myth-busting.md#myth-16-buying-more-tickets-dramatically-improves-odds)) |
| The app | Ticket checker, account | Lifecycle placements from the [mobile app touchpoints](../collateral/digital/mobile-app-touchpoints.md) set |

The placement rule: at retail, one idea per piece. Depth lives in the app and the [content hub](../collateral/digital/website-content-hub.md), one QR code away.

## Retail readiness

You train a network, not a staff. Materials do the work:

1. Ship the [staff FAQ](../collateral/customer-service/staff-faq.md) as a one-page counter card; a clerk should find any answer in ten seconds.
2. Keep the [stigma-free language](../messaging/stigma-free-language.md) list inside every retailer kit.
3. Give corporate-owned stores and claim centers the full [conversation scripts](../collateral/customer-service/conversation-scripts.md), including the Tier 2 handoff; high-value claims are the moment a real conversation can happen.

## Jurisdiction overlay

Lottery mandates are precise: odds statements on tickets, helpline text, age lines, and beneficiary disclosures. Open your [jurisdiction module](../jurisdictions/) next to this playbook; mandated wording wins every conflict, and the templates adapt around it. Modules tagged `lottery` carry the ticket-level detail.

## Measure it

Score deployments with the [content scorecard](../docs/content-scorecard.md). Lottery's tell is the surge test: compare odds-content reach in a normal week against a jackpot week. A program that scales its literacy with its queue is doing the job; one that goes quiet when the prize peaks is decoration.

## Start this week

1. Configure [_brand.yml](../_brand.yml) with your lottery's name and helpline.
2. Render the counter set: rack card, helpline card, and the lottery quick ref.
3. Put draw odds in the screen loop in plain numbers.
4. Schedule [Luck, Numbers & Lotteries](../messaging/campaigns.md#campaign-4-luck-numbers--lotteries) for the next surge window.
5. Ship the one-page FAQ card to the retail network with the next terminal update.

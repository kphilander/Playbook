Art direction / Photography

Show what comes next.

Photograph a specific moment in the rest of the evening: friends meeting for dinner, arriving at a show, or heading out together.

Direct an action
One friend joins the table; another turns to greet them. Use eye contact, a small gesture and an unfinished moment to give the scene a story.
Cast for the audience
Two or three clearly adult friends, with individual style and believable rapport. Let local people and places give each brand its character.
Light the people
Warm directional light, clear eyes and natural skin texture. Keep architecture quiet, with detail visible in the shadows and no bright strip behind the headline.
Compose for the cover
A full-bleed 4:5 portrait. Keep the upper 35% quiet, faces and the key gesture in the middle, and the bottom 24% low in detail. Supply native detail for a 3240 × 4050 crop.

Social Club: a restaurant terrace or show foyer, tactile materials and warm light. Circuit: adult friends heading out for late food, contemporary street light and a little more movement. The shared subject is the next plan together.

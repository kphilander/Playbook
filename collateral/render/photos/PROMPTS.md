# Poster Photography Prompts (19-series)

Photography for the 19-series posters is AI-generated with Google `gemini-3-pro-image`
(3:4 portrait, 2K output), art-directed to the spec in
[`visual-identity/photography/photography.md`](../../../visual-identity/photography/photography.md):
cinematic warm key light (~5500K), navy-tinted shadows, lifted blacks, diverse adults,
candid social energy, no alcohol, no readable screens, lower-third negative space for text.

To regenerate an image, rerun its prompt below and save over `poster-<id>.jpg`.

## poster-19a

```
Four well-dressed friends gathered at a roulette table in an upscale, modern casino lounge at night. A woman in the center is mid-laugh as she places a single casino chip on the felt; her three friends lean in around her, engaged and delighted. Smart-casual nightlife attire. Only a few scattered chips on the table — no tall chip stacks, no cash anywhere. Colorful casino lights render as soft out-of-focus glows deep in the background. Candid editorial lifestyle photograph, full-frame camera, 50mm lens at f/2.8. Cinematic intentional lighting: a clear, warm directional key light on the subjects' faces, with the background falling away into soft bokeh. Warm color temperature around 5500K with golden amber highlights; shadows tinted toward deep navy blue; blacks gently lifted, never crushed; medium-high contrast; natural, well-exposed skin tones. Genuine candid documentary energy — real mid-laugh expressions, no one looking at the camera, nothing posed or corporate. All people are clearly adults in their late twenties to mid forties, diverse in ethnicity and gender. No alcoholic drinks anywhere in the frame. No readable text, numbers, logos, or legible screen content anywhere in the frame. Vertical 3:4 portrait composition: subjects occupy the upper two-thirds of the frame, and the lower third falls off into darker, softly defocused tones — clean, quiet negative space suitable for a designer to place headline text.
```

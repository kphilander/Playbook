How this was made

A shared brief.
Independent authorship.

Each agent produced one sports betting concept, one gambling myth concept, and one concept of its choice. All five started in fresh contexts with the same dispatch instructions, required reading, output size, fonts, and renderer.

The models chose their topics, hooks, copy, and visual treatments. These are revised outputs after layout, contrast, and factual checks. The coordinator’s feedback is documented. Spark reported that it could not inspect images; the coordinator reviewed its renders.

Hiding names is a viewing aid, not a blind study: identities remain in files and have already been shown. A shared-input benefit must be tested against a credible brief-only condition within each model.

# Radio Spot Scripts

Production-ready scripts for {{PROGRAM_NAME}} radio advertising. 3 x 30-second spots and 2 x 15-second spots.

> **Operator note**: Replace all `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` tokens with values from `_brand.yml`. Speak the helpline clearly: twice in 30-second spots and once in 15-second spots. Voice talent should sound natural and conversational — not announcer energy. See [application guidelines](../../brand-book/07-application-guidelines.md#radio-and-audio) for audio guidelines.

> **Logo usage (companion visuals only)**: Radio spots are audio-only and have no on-screen logo. If companion digital banners or print ads run alongside radio spots, use the {{PROGRAM_NAME}} logo per the [logo system](../../brand-book/03-visual-identity.md#1-logo-system): reversed variant (white wordmark) on dark backgrounds, full-color variant on light backgrounds. Minimum size: 0.25" (print) or 24px (digital). Maintain 1x logo-height clear space. Co-branding with operator logo: vertical divider (neutral_300 `#A8A8C0`, 1px), {{PROGRAM_NAME}} logo no smaller than 60% of operator logo height.

## Voice direction (all spots)

| Property | Guideline |
|---|---|
| **Voice type** | Natural, conversational. Like a smart friend explaining something interesting. |
| **Energy** | The Playbook voice — confident, direct, a little knowing. Not announcer-read. |
| **Pace** | Moderate. Let the facts land. Don't rush the numbers. |
| **Not** | PSA tone. Medical disclaimer voice. Condescending. Dramatic. Urgent. |
| **Emphasis** | Marked with **bold** in scripts below. Light emphasis — don't overdo it. |
| **Helpline read** | Slow down for the number. Speak each digit clearly. Repeat once in 30-second spots. |

## Spot 1 — "The Math" (30s)

**Theme**: Odds literacy
**Tone**: Confident / Informative

```
(Music: light, confident; fade under voice)
VO: A payout has two parts: your stake, and your profit.
    At minus one-ten, a winning one-hundred-ten-dollar cash bet
    returns two hundred ten dollars. One hundred is profit.
    {{PROGRAM_SHORT_NAME}}. Know what the numbers mean.
    Free, confidential support: {{HELPLINE_NUMBER}}.
    That’s {{HELPLINE_NUMBER}}.
(Music out)
```

**Timing**: Target duration includes pauses and the fully spoken contact details. Time the resolved script with the voice talent; trim copy before increasing pace.

## Music / sound direction

| Property | Guideline |
|---|---|
| **Genre** | Modern, upbeat. Lo-fi, indie pop, or light electronic. |
| **Tempo** | 100–115 BPM |
| **Feel** | Lifestyle brand energy. Confident, forward-moving. |
| **Avoid** | Corporate jingles, somber piano, dramatic strings, casino sound effects |
| **Spots 1–3** | Full music bed, fades under VO within 2 seconds |
| **Spot 4** | Brief intro, fades quickly |
| **Spot 5** | Warmer, softer. Acoustic guitar or soft pad. Still modern. |
| **Helpline read** | Music drops to near-silence for the number. Clean, clear. |

## Production checklist

- [ ] Voice talent matches Playbook voice — natural, not announcer
- [ ] Helpline spoken clearly, twice per 30s spot, once per 15s spot
- [ ] Numbers paced with slight pauses between digits
- [ ] Music is licensed for radio broadcast
- [ ] Total time verified (30.0s / 15.0s — no overrun)
- [ ] {{PROGRAM_NAME}} mentioned by name at least once
- [ ] No clinical language (no "responsible gambling," "problem gambler," etc.)

# Singapore — Advertising and Promotion Rules

> **Operator note**: This is an implementation reference, not legal advice. Do not publish or launch a casino advertisement or promotion until the Gambling Regulatory Authority (GRA) has approved it or legal counsel has documented a specific regulatory exception.

> **Last verified**: 2026-08-09
> **Next review due**: 2026-11-09

---

## Scope and governing sources

This module covers advertising and promotions by licensed land-based casino operators in Singapore. It does not authorize advertising for unlawful remote gambling or another gambling product.

| Source | What to check |
|---|---|
| [Casino Control (Advertising) Regulations 2010](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/CCA2006-S86-2010) | Prior approval, permitted limited advertisements, location and content conditions, interviews, and media releases |
| [Casino Control Act 2006](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/CCA2006) | Casino regulation, age and exclusion framework, and responsible-gambling duties |
| [GRA: Responsible Gambling, Advertising & Promotion](https://www.gra.gov.sg/harm-minimisation/responsible-gambling-advertising-promotion) | Regulator overview of approval and responsible-gambling measures |
| [GRA statutes and regulations](https://www.gra.gov.sg/statues-regulations) | Current legislation and subsidiary instruments |
| [NCPG helpline and webchat](https://www.ncpg.org.sg/services/national-problem-gambling-helpline-and-webchat) | Current public help service and hours |

The consolidated Advertising Regulations were current as of July 2026 and include amendments commencing on 7 and 15 July 2026. Recheck the official version before each campaign.

## Prior approval is the default

Regulation 3 prohibits a casino operator from carrying out advertising or promotion without prior approval. Treat approval as a release gate, not a retrospective filing.

The submission and production record should preserve:

- the exact approved creative and copy;
- every format, size, language, channel, audience, placement, and flight date;
- the approval reference and any conditions;
- the owner responsible for stopping or replacing the campaign; and
- evidence that the published version matches the approved version.

Material copy, design, offer, audience, channel, or placement changes should return to compliance for a determination on reapproval.

## Limited advertisements

Regulation 4 permits limited advertising in specified circumstances, including certain transport gateways and designated tourist sites. The applicable categories include Changi Airport, Marina Bay Cruise Centre Singapore, the International Passenger Terminal, and sites designated under the Regulations.

A limited advertisement must stay within the regulatory test. It should be:

- factual, accurate, and capable of substantiation;
- not false or misleading;
- free of information about casino games or the amount or frequency of winnings; and
- free of an invitation or inducement to visit a casino or participate in gaming.

Do not infer that a tourist-facing venue or resort context is automatically eligible. Document the relevant category, location, content test, and legal review.

## Winnings, winners, and media activity

Outside casino premises, do not publish information about a patron's winnings or identify a winner. This restriction applies even when the story is framed as news, public relations, or social content.

Casino-related interviews and media releases are also controlled by the Regulations. Obtain prior approval unless the narrow impracticability process applies, then follow the required notification procedure. Escalate these items rather than treating them as ordinary corporate communications.

## Required disclosures and controls

Singapore uses an approval-based regime rather than one universal statutory ad script. The GRA approval, the operator's approved responsible-gambling programme, and the exact placement determine what must appear.

| Required element | Where to apply | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| **GRA approval record** | Every advertisement or promotion | Link the live asset to its approval reference, approved version, conditions, and flight dates |
| **Approved factual content** | Limited advertisements under regulation 4 | Publish only accurate, substantiable facts within the approved location and format |
| **Material offer terms** | Any approved promotion | State eligibility, duration, limits, and material restrictions prominently in the approved copy |
| **Responsible-gambling elements** | Where required by the approval or approved RG programme | Use the exact current age, support, and responsible-gambling elements; do not improvise after approval |
| **Current NCPG pathway** | Player-support material and any approved placement that calls for help information | Use 1800-6-668-668 and the current NCPG service details after confirming them at publication time |
| **Version and expiry control** | Every channel and language variant | Record the source version, approval owner, approval date, flight dates, and removal date |

## Content restrictions

Do not publish material that:

- lacks prior GRA approval or a documented regulatory exception;
- gives information about casino games, winnings, or winner frequency in a limited advertisement;
- invites or induces people to gamble through a limited advertisement;
- identifies a patron winner or publicises a patron's winnings outside casino premises;
- is false, misleading, or unsupported;
- targets people under 21, excluded people, or audiences inconsistent with the approval;
- presents gambling as a financial solution, social requirement, or way to recover losses; or
- differs materially from the approved copy, design, language, channel, audience, or placement.

## Responsible-gambling context

The GRA requires casino operators to maintain approved responsible-gambling programmes. Measures include pre-commitment facilities, employee training, player information, help services, and exclusion or visit-limit controls.

The National Council on Problem Gambling provides a confidential helpline and webchat daily from 8am to 11pm. The current published number is **1800-6-668-668**. NCPG also provides information about self-exclusion, family exclusion, third-party exclusion, and visit limits. Link to the [current NCPG service](https://www.ncpg.org.sg/services/national-problem-gambling-helpline-and-webchat) rather than copying changeable procedural detail into evergreen creative.

## Channel review

| Channel | Review question |
|---|---|
| Transport gateway or designated tourist site | Does the site qualify, and is every claim within the limited-advertisement test? |
| Casino or integrated resort | Does the approval cover this exact physical area, format, and audience? |
| Website, app, email, or social | Does the approval name the channel, audience, version, and flight dates? |
| Public relations or earned media | Is this an interview, media release, winnings story, or winner identification requiring escalation? |
| Promotion | Are all material terms in the approved creative and readable at the decision point? |

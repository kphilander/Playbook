# Compliance messages and creative freedom

Research date: 5 September 2026. Working research for creative planning; not an approved jurisdiction module or clearance of an individual campaign.

**A full-width bottom banner is not a universal requirement.** The requirements can concern the message, its prominence, an age notice, access to support, or a particular display treatment. Those are separate decisions. Playbook currently bundles several of them into one recurring footer.

**Calling a piece an “RG ad” does not automatically exempt it.** Some markets make specific accommodations for education or responsible-gambling campaigns. Their scope varies: permission to use an athlete, for example, does not also establish permission to omit a support message.

This review compares selected US states, Canadian requirements, Great Britain, Malta, Australia, Sweden and Brazil. It does not clear every market in the repository. Primary legislation, regulator publications and the actual industry codes were preferred over the existing jurisdiction summaries. Access or currency limitations are identified below.

## First classify the piece

Use the whole communication, including its destination, to make the classification. A helpful budgeting headline can still form part of a gambling promotion.

| Content | Questions that determine the treatment |
|---|---|
| Independent education | Who publishes and funds it? Does it promote an operator, gambling product or gambling participation, directly or indirectly? |
| Operator-funded education or RG campaign | Is the purpose exclusively educational? What do the logo, imagery, surrounding offers and destination communicate? Does a specific local exception cover this campaign? |
| Gambling promotion | Which operator, licence, product and market? Does it include an offer, acquisition CTA, product endorsement or invitation to play? |
| Support or account interface | Is the information required at a particular stage, such as registration, account access or self-exclusion? An interface obligation should not automatically become an every-ad obligation. |
| Hospitality or ordinary video-game content | Does it promote gambling at all? A shared visual style does not make the legal classification identical. Assess gambling features and cross-promotion separately. |

This is a proposed briefing framework, not a substitute for the definitions in each applicable rule. Branding alone is treated differently between markets.

It does **not** say every advertisement needs a bottom strip. | The repo’s citation does not establish its claimed universal ad-footer requirement. Separate advertising, account-interface and operator-code obligations still need checking. **Do not interpret this finding as an exemption for all Nevada advertising.** [NGC Regulation 5, §5.170](https://www.gaming.nv.gov/siteassets/content/regs/regulation-5-as-of-01-26.pdf). |
| **New Jersey — casino advertising** | N.J.A.C. 13:69C-14.2(c) prescribes help wording for print, billboards and signs; comparable language requires Division approval. On-site advertising has a separate messaging rule. Section 14.1 excludes certain required notices and approved gaming guides from this chapter’s advertising definition. | Legibility and approved wording matter; this section does not prescribe a bottom rectangle. A general educational post is not automatically an “approved gaming guide.” This row does not settle separate sports-wagering or internet-gaming rules. [DGE Chapter 69C, §§14.1–14.2](https://www.nj.gov/lps/ge/docs/Regulations/CHAPTER69C.pdf). |

## Currency and evidence limits

- **Massachusetts:** the accessible clean MGC text is dated June 2023. The 2026 hearing notice identifies a proposed removal of “branding” from §256.06(3), not a revision of the numeric display rules. The consolidated mass.gov text could not be retrieved, so final adoption/effective status was not established here. Use the sizing provisions as a documented planning baseline, not a blanket clearance. [MGC hearing notice](https://massgaming.com/wp-content/uploads/205-CMR-256.06-Notice-of-Public-Hearing.pdf).
- **Australia:** the Interactive Gambling Amendment (Gambling Reform) Act 2026 received assent on 26 August 2026. Its substantive schedules, except the transitional Schedule 5, commence on **1 January 2027**. The enactment should be tracked separately from today's implementation plan; it is no longer merely a proposed reform. [Act, commencement table](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00072/asmade/2026-08-26/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).
- **Ontario and Brazil:** the gaps are identified in their rows. Neither is classified as “no banner required.” Other repo markets and operator-specific licence conditions were not exhaustively reviewed.

## What the content actually needs to satisfy

For each proposed deployment, keep a short requirements record with these independent fields:

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Applicability | Market, product, operator/licence, audience, channel, paid/organic placement, campaign purpose and destination. |
| Required information | Age notice, exact or flexible RG message, support phone/URL, self-exclusion reference, licence identity, and offer terms where applicable. Do not assume every field is required everywhere. |
| Presentation | Minimum prominence, contrast, font height or area, position if specified, duration, spoken delivery, language, official-logo handling. |
| Permitted alternative | The exact clause supporting a destination link, shared page context, different message, or exemption; its conditions and the information it actually covers. |
| Authority and currency | Law/regulation, licence requirement, industry code, operator policy or Playbook preference; source section, checked date and campaign review status. |

Also review the main creative. An accurate warning cannot rescue a misleading claim, inappropriate audience targeting, or imagery that implies financial success through gambling. The CGA code, for example, separately addresses status claims and appeal to underage audiences. For the luxury and video-game directions, keep sophistication and playful form while assessing what the actual imagery communicates to the intended audience. [CGA Code, §§II–III](https://adstandards.ca/wp-content/uploads/CGA-Code-for-Responsible-Gaming-Advertising-2025-10-24.pdf).

## Recommended direction for Playbook

Replace the single visual assumption with **four selectable treatments**, driven by the requirements record. These are design proposals, not legal categories:

1. **Integrated information.** Put the required contact/message next to the main CTA, within a calm side panel, or in a readable editorial line. Preserve every applicable wording and prominence requirement.
2. **Support as the main content.** On a support campaign, give the contact details the primary hierarchy. Avoid repeating the same contact information in a second decorative footer unless a distinct requirement calls for it.
3. **Compact destination route.** Use a short, visible route to the required information only where the applicable provision permits it. Check the actual landing page and allowed click distance. A profile bio, caption or QR code is not automatically interchangeable with an on-image disclosure.
4. **Protected warning panel.** Reserve the prescribed message treatment for formats and markets that need it. Build the surrounding creative to complement that panel; preserve required colours, wording, size and timing.

A fifth comparison, **no additional compliance panel**, is useful only for a piece whose classification and applicable obligations support that outcome. It should never be a global “RG mode” switch.

For the preference gallery, compare identical copy with an editorial contact line, an adjacent support card, a dedicated support composition, and a protected warning panel. Label each as a **layout study** with its assumed market/format. Otherwise an aesthetic vote could be mistaken for permission to omit information.

## Where the repository needs clarification

- [Chapter 7](../brand-book/07-application-guidelines.md) explicitly specifies a persistent page footer and a helpline strip on every screen. That is a **Playbook application policy**. Its deeper principle—support easy to reach, readable and unobtrusive—can survive a more flexible implementation.
- The [production size matrix](../collateral/production-size-matrix.md) already moves support details to a destination for small banners. This should be conditional on market/format eligibility, rather than treated as a global exception.
- The [Nevada advertising module](../jurisdictions/united-states/nevada/advertising-rules.md) labels disclosures as required for all advertising while citing a venue-materials provision. Separate those contexts and identify any additional authority before relying on that table.
- The [Ontario advertising module](../jurisdictions/canada/ontario/advertising-rules.md) overstates the CGA code's nationwide applicability and presents illustrative helpline wording as mandatory. Its AGCO Standard 2.08 attribution also needs fresh verification.
- The current [preference specimens](../collateral/design-preferences/build.mjs) share an age/help footer for visual consistency. That treatment and the default contact information do not establish worldwide compliance.

No templates, authoritative jurisdiction modules, helpline defaults or production settings were changed as part of this research. The next creative revision can use the treatments above while keeping the distinction between an attractive layout and a cleared market-specific asset explicit.
